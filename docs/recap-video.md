# Animated awards reel

Finish all verdicts and open **The awards ceremony**. The new **Your tabs made a short film** panel turns the results into five chapters: the therapist's introduction, spotlight, group therapy, balcony, and final award.

- **Play reel** starts a 15-second captioned preview. It never starts automatically.
- **Pause reel**, **Restart reel**, chapter buttons, and the timeline let you revisit any moment.
- **Minimal motion** follows the session's reduced-motion preference initially. It removes entrances, bobbing, and rotation in both the preview and export. Chapter cuts and the progress indicator remain.
- **Save video** generates a 1200×630 silent WebM on this device in about 15 seconds. No voice provider is needed. The live session's ElevenLabs audio is not included.
- Keep the tab visible while exporting. **Cancel export**, switching away from the tab, or leaving the results stops recording and discards the partial film. Returning does not restart it automatically.
- Browsers without supported canvas recording keep the preview and PNG/SVG card downloads. WebM compatibility varies between social platforms; this release does not convert to MP4.

The renderer receives only aggregate verdict counts and authored award copy. It never receives tab names, URLs, IDs, history, or voice recordings. The clip uses a fictional cast with at most five representative characters per scene; displayed counts show the actual totals. It records an internally generated canvas, never your screen or microphone, and uploads nothing.

A playable example is generated by the browser tests at `artifacts/recap-film.webm`. Automated checks decode the downloaded file, verify its dimensions, seek into its final chapter, exercise pause and cancellation on desktop/mobile, and confirm canceled capture tracks stop. Physical playback in third-party editing or social apps remains a manual compatibility check.

Implementation references: [Canvas captureStream](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLCanvasElement/captureStream) and [MediaRecorder format detection](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaRecorder/isTypeSupported_static).
